How I decide what goes out under your name

A design background means the work gets judged by how it looks and reads to your customer, not just whether it ran.

Mindset

  • Customers and calls over likes and reach
  • Launch small, test, keep what works
  • Clarity over cleverness in every line

Ethics

  • You approve what publishes under your name
  • No fake reviews and no invented numbers
  • Your ad account and your data stay yours

Operating style

  • One clear call to action per piece
  • Reports in plain language, not dashboards
  • A human checks it before it goes live
§ 03

How we
work together

It starts with your numbers. You see every piece before it goes out, and you keep the accounts, the site and the data when we are done.

The process

Five stages, each with something you can see and sign off on.

01

Numbers call

A free call where we go through what you sell, what a customer is worth, and where they find you today.

02

Audit

I look at your site, your ads and your follow-up, and show you where customers drop off.

03

Build

Ads, pages, outreach or automation, in short rounds with a preview at each one.

04

Launch

It goes live once you approve it, with tracking in place so we know what each piece brings in.

05

Improve

Plain-language reports, then we cut what does not work and put more behind what does.
