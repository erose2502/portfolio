    § 06

    Before you
    book

    What owners usually ask before the first call.

    What does the free Meta ads audit check?

    It reads your page's active ads in Meta's public Ad Library and scores six things out of 100: how many ads you have in market, how fresh the creative is, the mix of video and images, whether you test different messages, where the ads run, and whether they lead to a clear next step. Spend and results aren't public, so it scores what is.

    How much does it cost?

    It depends on the plan and how many channels you want covered. Starter, Growth and Full Funnel are compared side by side on the plans page, and the first numbers call is free. Compare the plans

    What is Plaith?

    Plaith runs paid social on Facebook and Instagram. It launches new ads every week, keeps the winners running and replaces the rest. Every plan includes new AI videos each month, and a person checks every ad before it goes live. I'm a Plaith partner, so I set it up and run it with you.

    Do I keep my ad account and my data?

    Yes. The ad account and payment method stay yours and Plaith only gets partner access. Your ad budget goes to Meta directly. When we're done you keep the accounts, the site and the data.

    Do I have to sign a long contract?

    No. Plaith plans cancel monthly.

    Do I see the work before it goes live?

    Yes. Everything is built in short rounds with a preview at each one, and nothing goes live until you approve it.

    Do you work with agencies?

    Yes. I'm available for agency white-label work and one-off projects as well as the monthly plans. Book a call
