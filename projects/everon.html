Everon.

Rebuilt from prototype to production.

Everon is an intelligent AI career assistant that helps job seekers optimize resumes, prepare for interviews, and discover relevant opportunities. Built with OpenAI GPT-4, LiveKit voice integration, Firebase auth, and Firestore persistence.

Mindset applied

Career guidance should be practical, respectful, and measurable. Everon emphasizes actionable advice and user-owned progress.

  • Actionable feedback over generic advice
  • Clear boundaries on recommendations
  • Data ownership and privacy by default
Features

Comprehensive career AI,
from resume to offer.

01AI resume analysis

Upload a PDF resume and receive instant AI-powered feedback: professional summary, career level assessment, strengths, improvement areas, actionable recommendations, and ATS keyword optimization.

02AI career coaching

Interactive chat with an AI assistant for personalized career advice, interview preparation, job search strategies, and career development planning.

03Real-time voice coaching

Natural voice conversations with the AI career coach via LiveKit-powered audio streaming. Hands-free coaching with real-time STT and TTS.

04Job search integration

Search and discover job opportunities from LinkedIn and Indeed with AI-powered job matching based on your resume profile.

05Authentication & persistence

Firebase auth with email, password, and Google OAuth. Firestore persistence for conversation history, resume uploads, and session continuity across devices.

06Rebuilt from prototype

Everon was fully rebuilt from a stateless prototype into production architecture, demonstrating the ability to refactor and harden AI systems, not just greenfield build.
