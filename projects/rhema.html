Platform features

Full-stack discipleship,
AI at every layer.

01Biblical character AI

Users engage in dialogue with AI representations of biblical figures. Each character has a carefully engineered persona: distinct voice, knowledge scope, and theological grounding.

02Prayer journal

Daily prayer tracking with reflection prompts. Supabase Realtime keeps entries synced across devices instantly.

03Scripture reading plans

Structured Bible reading plans with progress tracking, devotionals, and AI-guided reflection questions tied to each passage.

04Multiplayer games & leaderboards

Faith-building interactive games with real-time multiplayer via Supabase Realtime channels. Scripture trivia, memory challenges, and community competitions.

05Community & messaging

Social hub with direct messaging, community posts, and an invitation system. Presence indicators via Supabase Realtime channels.

06Live voice & video calls

Audio and video calling UI powered by LiveKit for community prayer sessions and group discipleship calls.
