Kazi.

The execution OS for AI agents.

Kazi lets you define any business workflow, deploy a coordinated crew of specialized AI agents to execute it, and stay in control with a human checkpoint before anything goes out. It replaces the scattered stack of tools, manual handoffs, and one-off automations with a single place where your agents work, and you approve.
