Relay.

The coordination layer for voice AI agents.

Not just a conversational AI, but a system that turns phone calls into parallel, structured actions. A multi-tenant B2B platform dispatching parallel voice AI agents to coordinate complex transactions. It replaces sequential manual calls with parallel dispatch, extracting structured JSON into a unified dashboard.

Mindset applied

Voice AI needs accountability. Relay prioritizes measurable latency, grounded answers, and structured outcomes over flashy demos.

  • Grounded responses with tenant data
  • Structured post-call JSON for clarity
  • Fallbacks and safety checks baked in
What is working right now

Production-grade voice AI,
built for coordination at scale.

01Live inbound & outbound calls

Real PSTN numbers via LiveKit telephony. Agents answer instantly. Outbound campaigns dispatch to lead lists with configurable concurrency and calling-hour windows.

02Ultra-low latency pipeline

Silero VAD, OpenAI Whisper STT, GPT-4o-mini, and OpenAI TTS. Tuned for natural conversation with minimal perceptible delay.

03Post-call structured extraction

After every call, GPT-4o-mini parses the transcript into structured JSON: status, blocker, next_date, action_required, key_facts.

04RAG knowledge base

Per-tenant ChromaDB vector store. Agents answer questions grounded in your documents during the call, with no hallucinated facts.

05Transaction coordination

Dispatch coordination calls to all parties simultaneously, then surface blockers, key facts, and required actions per party.

06Agent builder

Custom voice agents with system prompts, voice selection, model choice, and temperature. Eight pre-built templates ready to deploy.

07Intent classification

GPT-4o at low temperature routes calls to the right disposition path, with automatic fallback to Twilio TwiML Gather if LiveKit is unavailable.

08Multi-tenant isolation

Per-tenant vector stores, agent configs, and call history. Complete data isolation between accounts with scoped API access.

The core insight

Phone tag is the bottleneck.

The problem

Professionals in real estate, insurance, healthcare, legal, and logistics spend hours every week making sequential phone calls to different organizations to coordinate a single transaction.

The change

Relay dispatches all those calls in parallel, extracts what was said into structured outcomes, and surfaces the results in a unified dashboard.

The result

What takes three days of phone tag takes minutes. Per-party results surface blockers, key facts, and required actions automatically.
